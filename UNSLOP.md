## Audit (before)

Level: light. Starlight gave it a sound structure and the ink and aqua palette fits the product; the signs were in the theme and the home page.

- Home hero: two radial color washes (aqua and coral) behind the page, and a teal drop-shadow glow on the hero image
- Hero headline "Messaging that just arrives." could fit any messaging product, with a trailing period
- Hero image: gradient wave strokes, traffic-light window dots and a bold filled "202 Accepted" pill
- Hero buttons as full pills
- Start here cards: an icon in a different colored tile on each card (orange, purple, green, red) and an accent border on hover
- 700 weights on the page title, hero headline and site title; -0.02em tracking on every heading down to h4
- Site title in the accent color
- Current sidebar page as an accent pill at weight 600
- API pages: a solid accent banner across the top, and the version badge in the sidebar as a filled accent tag
- Endpoint method labels as tinted pills at weight 700 with wide tracking
- Trailing periods on the short Popular guides descriptions

## Direction

- Product: documentation for the Tidewire SMS API, for developers sending texts, managing contacts and reading delivery webhooks
- Character: precise, calm, a little salt-air
- World: the tide table and the message log, deep sea ink with aqua for what got through
- Moments: the hero request card, a real POST with its 202 answer and the message's delivery trail
- Signature: the delivery trail (queued, sent, delivered) with only the delivered step in aqua
